FONTLOG for Rosmaryn Esoteric

Basic font information
Rosmaryn Esoteric is a font of esoteric symbols (zodiac, planets, asteroids,
astrological aspects, Yijing monograms, digrams, trigrams and hexagrams),
released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
